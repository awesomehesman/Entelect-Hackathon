# Simulation mechanics

This file separates what is explicitly specified in the official challenge materials from what is implied but not proven, and from what is unknown and requires empirical validation.

## Explicitly specified behaviour

- discrete ticks and day-based progression
- at most 20 planting actions per tick
- per-cell single-plant occupancy unless coexistence rules allow otherwise
- time-to-maturity before a plant becomes active
- spread_rate cadence and spread_type geometry
- spread_range
- root_type values: Shallow, Deep, None
- invasiveness_rank used to determine competition pressure
- preferred soil restrictions
- weaknesses and special rules
- plant unlock condition trees (AND/OR/NOT, coverage, count, event, feature, species presence/absence)
- season changes from the level file
- world events Rain, Drought, Ash Eclipse, Earthquake
- animal presence is dynamic and may disappear when conditions fail
- nutrient capacity starts at 100 and drains by 1 point per tick for occupied cells
- dead matter regains nutrients at 1 per tick capped at 100
- cells with dead matter lose nutrients at 0.5 per tick if a new plant occupies them
- shade state generated by mature plants with shade_radius
- burnt soil generated around Emberroot Trees
- paths are uninhabitable
- stone is uninhabitable; Stone Reed requires adjacency to stone
- cracks can form after earthquakes; Skyvine may spread over cracks
- final scoring uses only final state

## Implied behaviour

- spread order and collision resolution are partly defined, but the exact full update ordering is not explicit enough to implement without a simulator validation pass
- spread modifier priority when multiple conditional modifiers and animals interact is not fully defined in the official materials
- root-type interaction semantics beyond the unlock gating are not fully enumerated in the PDF
- subsurface growth, nutrient transfer networks and coexistence semantics require validation against the simulator
- precise earthquake crack generation is not fully specified by the challenge text

## Unknown behaviour requiring empirical validation

- exact tick processing order
- whether unlocks persist permanently or are dynamically re-evaluated
- exact handling when multiple plants spread into the same cell
- exact resolution of coexistence rules
- exact semantics of species count and scoring in presence of subsurface plants
- rounding semantics for spread multipliers and nutrient modifications
- exact adjacency definition for weakness rules
- exact lifetime accounting for the longevity score
- whether a plant can be manually planted on a preferred soil even if a weakness would immediately invalidate survival
- whether world events remain logically active or merely transpired for unlock conditions
- exact evaluation order of animals relative to unlock evaluation and plant spread
